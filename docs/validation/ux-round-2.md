# UX round 2 — Board: match the launcher's flat, dense board (issue #32)

Round 1 (#27) claimed board density and flat separators fixed; visual inspection against the reference — the fleet launcher's board column on a phone — showed they were not: the Board still read as stacked cards with big margins, the drawer title clipped on the phone. This round was judged **only by pixels**: iterate screenshot → compare against the reference → adjust, against a **disposable seeded instance** (synthetic data only), until the picture matched. Re-does part of #27.

After: [phone board](../screenshots/ux-round-2-1-phone.png) · [phone drawer + composer](../screenshots/ux-round-2-2-phone.png) · [desktop](../screenshots/ux-round-2-3-desktop.png).
