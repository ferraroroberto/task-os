# UX round 9 — the Archive tab fits a phone (issue #168)

Ninth on-screen review, and the first one filed from a phone rather than from a desktop: the **Archive** tab ([#159](https://github.com/ferraroroberto/task-os/issues/159)) shipped drawn for the desktop table and degraded badly at 390 px. The owner's own list, point by point, is [#168](https://github.com/ferraroroberto/task-os/issues/168); this round is that list.

Nothing about what the archive *does* changed — no API, no model, no threshold. The whole round is layout, wording at one width, and one state the screen was not showing.

Judged at 390×844 light and dark on the disposable instance over the **fake archiver** (`tests/fixtures/archiver_fake.py` — synthetic subjects, senders and folders, the only archive data allowed on screen in a public repo), then walked read-only on the **live instance** over its three real runs. Desktop re-checked at 1440×900 against the shots the same commit's parent produced.

Phone: [the head, the picker, the bulk button and a reviewed row](../screenshots/story-24-archive-7-phone.png) · [one card's review menu and its *File it…* panel, dark](../screenshots/story-24-archive-8-phone.png). Desktop, unchanged in substance: [the report](../screenshots/story-24-archive-4-desktop.png) · [the panel while filing](../screenshots/story-24-archive-5-desktop.png) · [after the review round, dark](../screenshots/story-24-archive-6-desktop.png).

## What deliberately did not change

- **The desktop.** The field and its picker are grouped in the markup so the phone can draw them as one box; on a wide screen `display: contents` dissolves the group and its two children are the panel grid's own first two tracks, exactly as before. The desktop keeps the two-column status rows, the full sentences, the `Accept all N that need you` label and the picker's `26rem` cap. The one deliberate desktop improvement is a `min-height` on the panel's picker button, which had been sitting half a line above the field it belongs to.
- **What the archive does.** No route, no schema, no model, no threshold, no new state — `decided_at` was already written by *Accept*; the screen simply reads it now.
- **The e2e suite size.** Story 24 gained two mails and a block of geometry assertions, no test: **16 collected across 13 files**, as before.
- **The vendored components.** Byte-identical (`design_lint vendored` → every component `IDENTICAL`).
